Controller controls

Native controls remain available with a mouse, keyboard, or touch.

  • A Play or pause; choose a setting
  • B Mute; close Settings
  • X Captions · Y Fullscreen
  • LB/RB/LT/RT Seek 10 seconds
  • B9 / ☰ Open Video.js Settings
  • B8 / Back Close Settings
  • D-pad / left stick Seek, volume, or navigate Settings
  • L3 Picture-in-picture · R3 Remote playback

Connect a controller and press a button to use gamepad controls.
